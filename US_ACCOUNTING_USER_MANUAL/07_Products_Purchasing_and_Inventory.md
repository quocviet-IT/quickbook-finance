# 07. Products, Purchasing, and Inventory

## Products and services

Items contain SKU, name, type, category, sales description, purchase description, price, cost, income account, expense or asset account, taxability, unit of measure, effective version, and status.

Duplicate SKUs are rejected. Inactive items remain visible on historical documents. Price or account overrides follow configured permission and approval rules.

## Purchase orders and receiving

Purchase orders use controlled numbering, approval thresholds, version history, expected dates, quantities, prices, and vendor terms. Partial receipt, rejection reason, backorder, manual closure, and bill conversion are traceable.

Three-way matching compares purchase order, receipt, and bill. Configured quantity or price variances outside tolerance enter an exception workflow.

## Optional inventory

When enabled, inventory uses a documented costing method and records every receipt, issue, transfer, return, and approved adjustment in a subledger.

Inventory quantity and valuation reconcile to ledger control accounts. Negative inventory, duplicate receipts, and backdated changes follow explicit policy.
