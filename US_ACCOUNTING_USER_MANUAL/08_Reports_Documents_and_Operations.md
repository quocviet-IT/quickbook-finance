# 08. Reports, Documents, Imports, and Operations

## Financial reports

Required reports include Trial Balance, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement, General Ledger, Journal, AR ageing, AP ageing, Sales Tax Liability, and Bank Reconciliation.

Reports show active filters, accounting basis, date/time generated, company context, and base currency. Users can drill down to journal entries and source documents. CSV/PDF exports preserve parameters and are auditable.

Saved and scheduled reports are a later phase and must preserve permissions, time zone, retries, delivery history, and reproducibility.

## Dashboard

The dashboard emphasizes actionable exceptions: overdue receivables, bills due, unreconciled bank items, pending approvals, failed imports or integrations, close blockers, and unusual balance conditions.

## Documents

Attachments use private object storage, file-type and size restrictions, collision-resistant hashes, malware-scan status, access logs, retention rules, and legal hold when configured. Issued documents preserve the template version used.

## Imports

Imports use staging rows, explicit mappings, validation, duplicate detection, exception review, control totals, approval, and idempotent posting. Invalid rows cannot cause partial financial posting.

## Backup and recovery

Operations documentation defines backup frequency, recovery objectives, restore procedures, company-data export, incident response, and periodic restore tests. Vendor backup alone is not treated as a tested recovery process.

## Support

In-product help links to versioned, owned knowledge articles. Support tickets protect screenshots and diagnostic data, retain status history, and never bypass product authorization for financial actions.
